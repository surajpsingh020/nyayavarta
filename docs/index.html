Nyayavarta

Multilingual AI Legal Assistant & GraphRAG Platform for Indian Citizens under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS, 2023)

● 105 Statutory Concordance Transitions ● 38 Landmark Supreme Court Precedents ● Neo4j Graph (515 Nodes, 1,030 Edges) ● 25/25 Endpoint Security Audit Pass

🏛️ Statutory Law Transitions

Complete concordance mappings bridging colonial penal and procedural codes (IPC, CrPC, IEA) into the modernized BNS, BNSS, and BSA frameworks.

🕸️ Knowledge Graph & RAG

Powered by Neo4j 5.18 with 515 nodes and 1,030 semantic edges (REPLACED_BY, APPLIES_SECTION, OVERRULES) for deterministic legal citations.

🛡️ Enterprise Guardrails

Pre-retrieval adversarial prompt injection defense, harmful intent refusal, and Data Leak Prevention (DLP) masking citizen Aadhaar and PAN numbers.

⚖️ Free Legal Aid Routing

Automated procedural routing to National Legal Services Authority (NALSA / DLSA) under Section 12 with 24x7 toll-free helpline (15100).
